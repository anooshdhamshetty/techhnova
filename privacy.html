DATA TRANSPARENCY

Privacy Policy. Respecting your personal data.

This Privacy Policy explains the specific information practices of TechhNova. We maintain a minimal, privacy-first website architecture and do not engage in invasive tracking or data brokerage.

01 / GENERAL OVERVIEW

Our Privacy Commitment

TechhNova is an independently operated static website hosted on Vercel. We do not require user account registration, do not maintain user profile databases, and do not sell personal information to third parties.

This policy outlines what happens when you visit our website, when you communicate with us via email, and when you navigate to third-party software websites through our links.

02 / DIRECT COMMUNICATIONS

Information You Voluntarily Provide

Email Correspondence: When you send an email to TechhNova or our founder (e.g., at anooshdhamshetty@gmail.com), you choose what information to disclose (such as your name, email address, job title, and message contents).

We use this information exclusively to review your message, respond to your inquiry, evaluate editorial suggestions, or resolve reported corrections. We do not add your email address to promotional mailing lists without your explicit opt-in consent, nor do we share your correspondence with advertisers.

03 / TECHNICAL LOGS

Hosting Infrastructure & Server Logs

TechhNova is deployed on Vercel's global edge network. Like virtually all web servers, Vercel may temporarily process standard HTTP request logs (such as IP addresses, browser user-agent headers, referring URLs, and timestamps) solely for operational reliability, DDoS protection, and security debugging. These basic server logs are handled under Vercel's data security standards and are not merged with personally identifiable profiles.

04 / COOKIES & AFFILIATE PIXELS

Cookies, Analytics, and Affiliate Tracking

First-Party Cookies: The TechhNova website does not use cookies for visitor profiling, cross-site tracking, or behavioral advertising.

Third-Party Affiliate Links: When you click on an external link to a software vendor or affiliate partner (including partners on networks such as PartnerStack), the destination service may place a session cookie or tracking parameter in your browser. This cookie records that the referral originated from TechhNova to allow the vendor to credit commissions on qualifying purchases. These cookies are subject to the third-party provider's privacy policy.

You can disable or clear cookies at any time through your browser's privacy settings without affecting your ability to browse TechhNova.

05 / EXTERNAL DESTINATIONS

Links to Third-Party Websites

Our guides, comparisons, and directory include links to external websites, including official product documentation, open-source repositories, and software checkout pages.

TechhNova does not control the privacy practices, security protocols, or content of third-party platforms. When you leave our site, we encourage you to review the privacy notices of any software service before creating an account or entering sensitive payment details.

06 / SPECIAL PROTECTIONS

Children's Online Privacy

TechhNova is intended for students, developers, and general technology audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information via email, please contact us immediately, and we will promptly delete the communication.
