OUR EDITORIAL METHODOLOGY

Research first.
Recommend responsibly.

TechhNova is an emerging technology publication built on intellectual rigor. We do not accept sponsored reviews, sell top rankings, or publish unverified marketing claims.

01 Desk Research & Specification Audit We analyze documentation, API changelogs, pricing tables, and security whitepapers directly from official vendor sources.
02 Practical Hands-On Testing Where feasible, we deploy tools in real engineering and writing workflows to measure latency, usability, and edge-case errors.
03 Limitation Reporting Every listing and review includes explicit drawbacks and trade-offs. No software product is universally perfect.
04 Transparent Disclosures Affiliate relationships support our independent publication but never compromise our editorial objectivity.
