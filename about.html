OUR STORY

A curious mind.
A world of technology to explore.

TechhNova is an independent initiative built around discovering useful software, understanding emerging technology and sharing what we learn.

WHO WE ARE

Technology should feel more approachable.

TechhNova was founded by Anoosh Dhamshetty in Hyderabad, India. It is a new, independently run technology discovery initiative focused on AI tools, SaaS products, developer resources, productivity software and education technology.

We believe people should be able to understand what a digital product does, who it may be useful for and what to consider before choosing it. Our aim is to build that understanding one topic at a time.

OUR MISSION

Make technology discovery clearer and more useful.

We want to help beginners, students and technology enthusiasts explore digital tools with more confidence. We are starting small, learning continuously and developing content as we gain practical understanding.

TechhNova is currently growing its online presence. We do not claim an established audience, customer base or history of product reviews.
