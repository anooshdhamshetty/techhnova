OUR ORIGIN & IDENTITY

A curious mind. An independent technology publication.

TechhNova is an emerging, independent technology publication dedicated to discovering, analyzing, and explaining digital software with clarity, intellectual honesty, and zero promotional fluff.

WHO WE ARE & WHY WE EXIST

Making technology choices clearer and more accessible.

TechhNova was founded by Anoosh Dhamshetty in Hyderabad, Telangana, India. We are an emerging platform focused on five primary technology pillars: Artificial Intelligence Tools, SaaS Products, Developer Technologies, Productivity Software, and Education Technology.

Modern software discovery has become exhausting. Search engines are saturated with automated affiliate pages that praise every tool equally, hide pricing traps, and invent fake review metrics. For a student trying to choose a study aid, a software developer comparing coding assistants, or a startup founder choosing a CRM, finding balanced, verifiable information is harder than ever.

TechhNova was established to provide an honest alternative: a grounded, independent publication that takes the time to read documentation, test features, audit pricing models, and highlight product limitations before making a recommendation.

01 / INTEGRITY

Intellectual Honesty

Every tool has trade-offs. We explicitly document limitations, price increases, and learning curves alongside positive features.

02 / CLARITY

No Marketing Buzzwords

We explain complex AI architectures and software paradigms in straightforward, practical language that respects your time.

03 / INDEPENDENCE

Uncompromising Independence

We do not sell rankings or accept sponsored reviews. Affiliate links support our operations but never determine our conclusions.

Who TechhNova Is Built For

We create research and guides tailored specifically to individuals and teams navigating software choices:

  • Students & Beginners: Learners discovering how generative AI and note-taking software can aid revision without violating academic integrity.
  • Software Developers: Engineers looking for honest benchmarks on AI coding assistants, database backends, and terminal workflows.
  • Technology Enthusiasts: Individuals passionate about keeping pace with emerging AI advancements and digital software tools.
  • Solo Professionals & Freelancers: Independent consultants optimizing task management, time tracking, and client communications.
  • Startups & Small Businesses: Founders evaluating SaaS platforms, auditing Total Cost of Ownership (TCO), and avoiding vendor lock-in.
