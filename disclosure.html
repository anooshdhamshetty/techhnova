COMMERCIAL TRANSPARENCY

Clear information. Honest relationships.

We believe visitors should understand exactly how this publication sustains itself financially, how affiliate referral links operate, and how we protect our editorial independence.

01 / AFFILIATE RELATIONSHIPS

What Are Affiliate Links & How Do They Work?

TechhNova participates in affiliate marketing programs, including partnerships facilitated through networks such as PartnerStack and direct software vendor affiliate programs.

This means that some outbound links published on our website are unique tracking links. When you click an affiliate link on TechhNova and subsequently sign up for a trial, create an account, or purchase a paid subscription, the software provider may pay TechhNova a referral commission.

The Zero Additional Cost Guarantee: Using an affiliate link on TechhNova never increases the price you pay. In many instances, affiliate partnerships allow us to provide verified student discounts, extended free trials, or exclusive onboarding terms negotiated directly with the vendor.
02 / INDEPENDENCE GUARANTEE

Affiliate Commissions Do Not Determine Our Coverage

Our core mission is to help our audience make better digital choices. We adhere to strict internal guidelines regarding affiliate monetization:

  • Relevance and Quality First: Products are selected for review and inclusion in our directory based on practical utility, engineering quality, and value for our readers—never on whether an affiliate program exists or what percentage commission it pays.
  • Free & Open-Source Tools Included: We regularly feature, review, and recommend free, non-monetized, or open-source software (such as Obsidian, Khan Academy, and VS Code) where no commercial compensation is received.
  • Unbiased Reviews: If an affiliate partner's product has high latency, aggressive pricing cliffs, or privacy drawbacks, we will explicitly state those limitations in our review. An affiliate relationship will never prevent us from publishing a critique.
  • No Paid Editorial Placements: Software companies cannot pay to be placed at the top of our comparison tables or to purchase a favorable review.
03 / READER NOTICES

How We Disclose Affiliate Links Across Our Site

In compliance with the United States Federal Trade Commission (FTC) guidelines, the Advertising Standards Council of India (ASCI), and international consumer protection standards:

  • Every editorial article, comparison guide, and review that includes affiliate links features a prominent disclosure callout at the beginning of the text, before any affiliate link is clicked.
  • The software discovery directory on explore.html displays an overarching disclosure banner indicating that outbound buttons may be referral links.
  • The website footer across every single page contains a permanent summary disclosure linking directly to this policy.
04 / CONTENT VOLATILITY

Product Information and Pricing Volatility

Software-as-a-Service companies continuously update their features, security tiers, free allowances, and billing terms. While we conduct regular audits and display a "Last Verified Date" on our reviews, we cannot guarantee that a vendor will not alter their terms after our review is published.

We encourage all visitors to confirm final contract pricing, renewal terms, and feature availability directly on the provider's official checkout page before completing a purchase.

05 / PRIVACY & TRACKING

Affiliate Cookies and Tracking Notice

When you click on an external affiliate link, the third-party software vendor or affiliate network (such as PartnerStack) may deposit a tracking cookie or pixel in your browser to record that you arrived via TechhNova. These cookies are governed by the respective third-party vendor's privacy policy.

TechhNova itself does not deploy behavioral profiling cookies or sell personal data. For complete details on our data protection practices, please read our dedicated Privacy Policy.
