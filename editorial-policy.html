INTEGRITY & RESEARCH STANDARDS

Editorial Policy. How we research and evaluate.

TechhNova is an emerging, independent technology publication. We believe readers deserve complete transparency regarding how digital products are chosen, how claims are verified, and how we maintain editorial independence from affiliate partners.

01 / GUIDING PHILOSOPHY

Our Core Editorial Commitment

Our mission is simple: Help students, software engineers, and small businesses make smarter digital choices through transparent, verifiable research.

We operate in an online media landscape cluttered with automated AI content, fake review scores, and pay-to-play rankings. TechhNova was built on the principle that trust is earned through factual accuracy, clear disclosures, and an honest willingness to highlight product flaws.

Key Editorial Principle: We will never present a software product as flawless. Every tool has trade-offs, pricing catches, or learning curves. Clearly communicating those limitations is our primary duty to our readers.
02 / SELECTION CRITERIA

How Software Products Are Selected

With thousands of SaaS and AI tools launched annually, we apply a strict filtering process before featuring any tool on TechhNova:

  • Practical Utility: Does the product solve an authentic, repeatable operational problem for students, developers, or professionals, or is it merely a superficial wrapper around public APIs?
  • Active Development & Stability: Is the company actively maintaining its codebase, releasing changelogs, and addressing user feedback? We prioritize stable, actively updated software over abandoned experiments.
  • Pricing Transparency: Does the vendor publicly publish its pricing tiers, or does it obscure basic costs behind high-pressure "Contact Sales" barriers?
  • User Privacy & Security: Does the provider publish an accessible privacy policy, outline encryption standards, and respect customer data sovereignty?
03 / EVALUATION METHODOLOGY

Distinguishing Hands-On Testing from Desk Research

We never misrepresent our evaluation process. To ensure complete intellectual honesty, our reviews and directory listings explicitly differentiate between two tiers of evaluation:

Hands-on Tested

Direct Practical Testing

The tool has been directly installed, configured, and tested in real-world workflows by our founder or editorial contributors. We evaluate setup friction, real UI latency, export reliability, and error handling.

Researched Spec

Rigorous Desk Research

The tool's features, APIs, and tiers have been cross-verified against official vendor documentation, API changelogs, security whitepapers, and verified user community technical feedback without full internal deployment.

We never claim to have completed hands-on testing on an enterprise tool or infrastructure platform unless that testing has genuinely taken place.

04 / PRICING ACCURACY

Pricing Verification Standards

SaaS pricing is notoriously volatile. Vendors frequently alter seat minimums, free tier allowances, and renewal terms. To protect our readers:

  • Every price cited on TechhNova is manually cross-checked against the vendor's official pricing page and terms of service.
  • We clearly note whether a price requires annual upfront prepayment or applies to monthly billing.
  • Every review card and article displays a "Last Verified Date" (e.g., November 2026).
  • We actively investigate "hidden costs," including seat minimums, overage consumption fees, Single Sign-On (SSO) premiums, and data egress charges.
05 / INDEPENDENCE & AFFILIATE RELATIONS

Affiliate Relationships & Advertising Separation

TechhNova participates in select affiliate partner programs, including networks like PartnerStack. This allows us to earn small commissions when readers choose to purchase software subscriptions through our referral links.

However, we maintain a strict wall between editorial judgment and commercial monetization:

  • No Paid Placement: We do not sell rankings, "Best Of" badges, or guaranteed positive reviews to software vendors.
  • Commission Neutrality: A higher affiliate commission rate will never cause a tool to be recommended over a more capable or affordable alternative.
  • Free Tier Advocacy: Where a free or open-source software alternative (such as Obsidian or Khan Academy) provides superior value over a paid affiliate product, we will prominently recommend the free solution.
  • Clear Disclosures: Every article or directory page containing affiliate links carries an explicit disclosure notice near the top of the content.
06 / ACCOUNTABILITY

Corrections and Updates Protocol

We take factual accuracy seriously. If a vendor changes its feature set, deprecates an API, alters its pricing tiers, or if we make an unintentional error, we welcome and encourage public corrections.

How to Request a Correction:
If you notice an outdated price, incorrect specification, or broken link, please email our founder directly at anooshdhamshetty@gmail.com with the subject line "Editorial Correction: [Article Title]". Please include links to the official documentation or release notes. We audit and resolve verified corrections within 48 business hours.
